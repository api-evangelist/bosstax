Yes — cybersecurity insurance premiums are fully deductible for independent IT consultants, since this coverage protects specifically against risks tied directly to the work you're paid to do, like a data breach on a client's system or liability from a security incident you're involved in. As an IT consultant, you often have access to sensitive client systems and data, which creates real exposure if something goes wrong — even through no clear fault of your own.
